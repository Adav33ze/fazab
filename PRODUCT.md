# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

FAZAB serves clients planning consequential building and infrastructure work in Nigeria: property developers, government agencies, corporate organisations, educational institutions, hospitality developers, investment groups, and private homeowners. They are evaluating whether one experienced partner can coordinate design, technical work, construction, and delivery with clarity.

## Product Purpose

The website establishes confidence in FAZAB International Limited and turns that confidence into qualified project enquiries. It should let prospective clients understand the practice, see its breadth, and choose a direct contact route without wading through a company profile.

## Positioning

FAZAB is a multidisciplinary design and construction practice that integrates architecture, technical services, construction, and project delivery from concept to completion.

## Operating Context

Visitors are often comparing firms for residential, commercial, corporate, hospitality, institutional, government, or infrastructure work. Their decision depends on credibility, experience, capability, quality, trust, and clear access to the right team.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The four service pillars are Architecture & Design, Construction & Infrastructure, Project Delivery, and Surveying & Technical Services.
- The site uses Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS and is intended for Vercel deployment.
- Keep the experience performant, responsive, keyboard-accessible, and WCAG AA-minded, with reduced-motion support.
- Preserve real contact routes for email, phone, and WhatsApp.
- Project records currently contain names and metadata but placeholder descriptions and no approved project galleries; the site must not turn those gaps into invented claims.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: FAZAB International Limited.
- Established in 1998 and headquartered in Abuja, Nigeria.
- Brand promise: “Constructing Possibilities. Redefining Excellence.”
- Voice: concise, direct, professional, honest, intelligent, calm, and confident; avoid hype, buzzwords, exhaustive service lists, and empty superlatives.
- Preserve the existing FAZAB logo asset.

## Evidence on Hand

- Brand and business guidance: `fazab.md`, `PROJECT.md`, and `branding/`.
- Existing mark: `public/brand/fazab-logo.svg`.
- One approved construction image is available in two duplicate files under `public/media/images/home/`.
- Three clearly labelled illustrative architectural studies are available under `public/media/images/studies/`; they are visual direction assets, not project evidence.
- Six project records exist in `src/data/projects.json`; their names, locations, disciplines, and years may be shown, but descriptions are explicitly placeholders and images are absent.
- Verified contact details are in `src/data/contact.json`.

## Product Principles

- Show work and delivery evidence before marketing language.
- Make integration visible: one practice, four connected disciplines, one line of accountability.
- Communicate quiet confidence through precision and restraint.
- Prefer curated, outcome-led information over completeness.
- Never fabricate proof to fill a content gap.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The experience must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks and headings, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and `prefers-reduced-motion`.
